Images can explain a process, illustrate a report or identify a document. EDTRDocs lets you insert an image into the document and adjust its size without first moving the writing into a desktop app.

## Place the image with its explanation

1. [Open the editor](/pad) and put the cursor near the paragraph that discusses the image.
2. Use the image insertion control to choose a file from your device, or paste an image from the clipboard when your browser supports it.
3. Select the inserted image and resize it to fit the page.
4. Add a short explanatory paragraph below it, including a source credit when appropriate.

Keep the original image separately if you need its original resolution. Images may be compressed during insertion to manage document size. A document export should not be treated as your only archive of the source photograph.

## Make the document easier to read

Use images that support the text. Avoid placing essential instructions only inside a screenshot: write the steps as text too. This helps readers who cannot see the image clearly and makes the document easier to search.

For information with repeated values, a [table](/guides/insert-table-online-editor) may be clearer than a screenshot of a spreadsheet. For a report, organize the surrounding text with [headings](/guides/rich-text-editor-online).

## Review before sending

Export a DOCX for further editing or a PDF for distribution. Check that the image stays within the page and that nearby paragraphs wrap sensibly. Imported and exported documents can differ in layout; follow the [DOCX checks](/guides/open-docx-online) and [print-preview steps](/guides/print-document-browser) when presentation matters.
