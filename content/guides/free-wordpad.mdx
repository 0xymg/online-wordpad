If you are looking for a free WordPad-style editor, EDTRpad offers browser-based writing and document export. It is a separate product from the former Microsoft Windows WordPad.

## What you can do

[Open the editor](/pad) to draft a letter, organize notes or edit a supported DOCX. Formatting includes headings, lists, tables and images. Use the toolbar for visible controls or `/` for the command menu.

Guest writing does not require an account. Account sync is available when you sign in, while guests keep saved documents in the current browser. The [no-login guide](/guides/wordpad-no-login) explains why you should still export important work.

## What free does not tell you

A free tool can still have compatibility limits. EDTRpad imports DOCX content, but complex layouts may change. It is not a live collaboration workspace and does not directly import every historical WordPad format.

Compare the actual task before switching: [Word alternatives](/guides/online-word-alternative) for Office documents, or the [WordPad replacement checklist](/guides/wordpad-replacement) for an older file collection.

## Keep a finished copy

Choose **File → Export** after writing. DOCX is suitable for further edits; PDF is suitable for distributing finished pages; TXT keeps only plain text.

Reopen your chosen format and check the result. The [DOCX export guide](/guides/export-to-word-docx-online) gives the exact handoff steps. There is no need to predict future pricing to decide whether today’s editor fits the document in front of you.
