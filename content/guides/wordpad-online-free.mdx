You can use EDTRDocs as a free online WordPad for everyday documents. Start in the browser, format your writing and export a copy without installing a desktop word processor.

## Try a real task first

Instead of testing every feature, create the kind of document you actually need: a short letter, a meeting summary or a one-page report. Add a heading, two paragraphs and a list, then export it.

The [first-document guide](/guides/how-to-use-wordpad-online) gives you a repeatable workflow. If you already have a DOCX, use the [opening guide](/guides/open-docx-online) to check it before editing.

## Separate saving from exporting

Guest saving keeps work in the current browser. Account sync makes your documents available across devices when signed in. Export creates a separate file outside those saved copies.

For an important document, use all the steps your situation needs: save, export and verify. Closing a tab is not a backup, and clearing browser storage may remove a guest document.

## Check whether it fits the job

EDTRDocs is useful for solo writing. It does not provide live coauthoring or guarantee exact conversion of complex Word layout. The [Google Docs alternative guide](/guides/google-docs-alternative) covers collaborative needs, while the [Word comparison](/guides/wordpad-vs-microsoft-word) covers more demanding Office work.

When the document is ready, follow [DOCX export](/guides/export-to-word-docx-online) or [printing](/guides/print-document-browser) according to what the recipient needs.
