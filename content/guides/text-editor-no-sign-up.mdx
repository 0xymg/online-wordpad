A no-sign-up editor is convenient when you need to write now and do not want to create another account. EDTRpad offers guest editing for that task, with an optional account for document sync later.

## A practical shared-computer workflow

[Open the editor](/pad), write the document and download a copy before you leave. Use a clear filename so you can find it among other downloads. Open the exported copy to verify the text and formatting.

Guest autosave belongs to that browser profile. It is not a private session that automatically erases everything when you close a tab. Remove documents you should not leave behind and follow the device owner’s rules for downloaded files.

## Decide how much formatting you need

For text you will paste into an email or another form, keep the document simple and export TXT if needed. For a letter, use headings and paragraphs and export DOCX or PDF.

The [simple-editor guide](/guides/simple-text-editor) covers minimal formatting. The [export guide](/guides/export-to-word-docx-online) explains how an editable file differs from the saved browser document.

## Know what no sign-up does not mean

It does not mean unlimited browser storage, automatic backups or guaranteed offline access. Load the editor with a connection and keep a separate copy of important work.

If you will move between devices, consider account sync or transfer files yourself. The [guest-saving guide](/guides/wordpad-no-login) describes the trade-offs in more detail.
