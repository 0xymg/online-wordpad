EDTRDocs is a free online word processor you can use without installing a desktop app. You still download a file when you export your finished work; “no download” refers to getting started with the editor.

## Work within the browser

[Open EDTRDocs](/pad), write a draft and format it with headings, lists and paragraphs. Tables and images are available when the document needs them. An account is optional for writing and adds sync when you sign in.

If this is your first document, follow the [step-by-step walkthrough](/guides/how-to-use-wordpad-online). Keep a network connection available to load the app rather than assuming offline startup will work.

## Bring a Word document with you

Use **File → Open** to select a DOCX. Review the converted content before editing; the import does not promise identical pagination or advanced Word features.

The [DOCX opening guide](/guides/open-docx-online) lists the checks. EDTRDocs does not directly open legacy DOC, RTF or password-protected Word files through a password-unlock workflow.

## Finish with the right format

Export DOCX for further editing, or PDF for a document intended to be read. Guest work stays in the current browser, so the export is also your portable copy.

For a fair comparison with Microsoft’s free web apps and desktop tools, read the [online Word alternative guide](/guides/online-word-alternative). Cost alone is not the deciding factor: collaboration, compatibility and offline access can matter more.
